Rabbit Hop

Solo exploration · Atlanta · 2026

Experiment

Can an AI agent make decisions like a specific kind of shopper, and can we prove it? I gave agents five researched personas, backed by 100 synthetic purchase histories, then had them browse Sephora and Olive Young pages and tracked how each one chose, step by step. A blind judge then guesses the persona from its choices and scores how consistent the runs are. The goal: test whether agents could stand in for early customer discovery.
